Grand Theft Auto: Vice City

reVC compiled to WebAssembly. Runs your own installed copy.

Nothing is downloaded and nothing is uploaded — the files are read from your disk and stay on this device. Needs models/gta3.img and data/gta_vc.dat.

Engine: reVC, a reverse-engineered reimplementation of the Vice City engine — every line of game logic is theirs. Browser port and Emscripten build: origami-ltd/wasm-revc (MIT, © 2026 Erasmo Bellumat / Origami 限). This page and its on-screen controls are ours.
No game data is distributed here. Vice City itself is Rockstar's, and you bring your own copy.
